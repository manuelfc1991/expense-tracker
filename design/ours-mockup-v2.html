Re-stepped for the dark surface and machine-checked, not eyeballed: lightness band, chroma floor, colour-blind separation and contrast all pass against #131318. Light

The same three screens, same structure. Semantic and category hues are stepped separately for the paper surface and machine-checked against it — a dark palette dropped onto white fails contrast and turns muddy.

Same tokens as the shipped app. Nothing here needs a new colour or typeface — what changed is hierarchy, alignment, and deciding what deserves to be large.
