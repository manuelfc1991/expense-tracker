Ours — the mark

The last two sets were pictures of things: a house, arrows, a percent sign, hands. None of them came from this app. This one is cut from the app itself — the character is the app's own typeface, pulled out of ours_mono_bold.ttf, and the rule under it is the same hairline every screen in Ours is printed on. It is the figure and the rule that Home already shows above everything else, at icon size.

The mark

The alternative, and the one I rejected

no rule
the letter O

Without the rule it is cleaner and says less — just a currency. With the rule it is a total on a statement, which is what this app is.

The O of OURS was the other candidate, and it is the one I would have picked on paper. It fails for a reason worth stating: at icon size O reads as zero, and zero is the worst number a money app can lead with.

Hands — fourteen attempts, and why none shipped

Crossed hands

Two crossed lines make an X, and an X means wrong, close, delete. On a home screen beside a bank app this reads as an error, not a ledger.

Crossed, with a note

Adding money at the crossing makes it worse — now it is a crossed-out figure.

Handshake

At 48px a handshake loses its fingers and becomes a wrench.

Cupped hands

The best of fourteen hand attempts. At 28px cupped hands become a bowl; it is handsome and it is not hands.

Two honest findings from the whole exercise. A disc above a curve is a face — the first cupped-hands attempt was a smiley, and no amount of adjustment removed it until the coin became a bar. And crossed hands cannot escape the X: the shape carries "wrong" so strongly that it overrides whatever the hands were meant to say.

Hands are also the house style of every charity and savings app there is, and they speak a different language from this one — which is hairlines, mono type and printed rules. If you still want hands I will build the best of them properly, but I would be doing it against my own advice, and you should know that before you ask.
