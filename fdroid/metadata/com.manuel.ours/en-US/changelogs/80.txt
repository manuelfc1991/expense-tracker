A credit card is now recognised from what its message says, not only from who sent it. The ICICI card texts from a header the app read as an ordinary bank, so on a phone where nobody had corrected it by hand the card appeared under what is left — money owed, counted as money to spend. It now reads "Credit Card" and the digits beside it, and files the card itself.

The digits have to sit right next to those words. A bill reminder can mention a credit card while naming the account it will be debited from, and treating that account as a card would report a real balance as debt.

Moving money into a card no longer inflates what left your accounts. A card issuer's message about being paid reads as money going out, so an amount that arrived was being counted as an amount that left — and the figure differed depending on whether the card had texted at all.

Deleting one half of a move now says both halves will go, because both always did. Transfers no longer offer to be recorded as refunds, and a move between two accounts with long names is readable again.
